## Context Scout AI

### X / LinkedIn

Built a CLI that turns a repo or PR diff into a compact brief for AI coding agents.

It ranks important files, boosts changed files with `--diff`, respects ignore patterns, and writes a token-budgeted Markdown brief.

Repo: https://github.com/subhanA-UA/context-scout-ai
